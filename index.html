EV companion app concept

A proposed mobile app for everyday electric riders. It answers four questions quickly: how much battery is left, how far can I go, where can I charge, and will this trip work?

  • Battery status: one clear number and colour state.
  • Estimated range: shown as a distance, with the assumption behind it.
  • Charging stations: nearby options sorted by distance.
  • Trip planning: says plainly if a charge stop is needed.

My design process

The method I follow when planning a feature.

  1. Understand the person

    Write down who uses it, what they are trying to do, and what goes wrong today.

  2. Define the problem

    Turn it into one clear sentence and a few testable requirements.

  3. Sketch and structure

    Plan the flow and data first, then the screens.

  4. Build and use AI carefully

    Prefer predictable logic where it works; add machine learning where it helps, and show the reasons behind its output.

  5. Test and refine

    Check on mobile and with a keyboard, fix what confuses, and write down what I learned.
